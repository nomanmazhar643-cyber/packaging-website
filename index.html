Quick answers about ordering, printing, payment and delivery.

Request a Quote Ask on WhatsApp
How do I place an order?

Add the products you need to your cart with the quantity, fill in your name, phone and city, choose a payment method and tap Place Order on WhatsApp. Your order comes to us on WhatsApp and we reply with the final price.

Why are there no prices on the website?

Custom packaging is priced by size, material, quantity and printing, so every order is different. Send us your order or a quote request and we'll reply on WhatsApp with the exact price.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Bank Transfer, JazzCash, Easypaisa and Cash on Delivery. No payment is taken on the website. Once the price is confirmed, we send you the payment details on WhatsApp.

Can you print my logo on the packaging?

Yes. Our products can be made plain or printed with your logo and brand colors. Send us your logo or design on WhatsApp or by email when you order.

Can I order a custom size?

Yes. Share the dimensions (length × width × height) and what you are packing, using the quote form or WhatsApp, and we'll suggest the right option.

Is there a minimum order quantity?

It depends on the product and whether it is printed. Tell us the quantity you need and we'll confirm what is possible in your quote.

How long does production take?

Production time depends on the product, quantity and printing. We give you a timeline together with your quote, before you confirm the order.

Do you deliver outside Multan?

We are based in Multan, Pakistan. Add your city and address when you order and we'll confirm the delivery options and charges for your location.

Do you offer eco-friendly packaging?

Yes. Many of our products are paper-based, such as kraft cups, cardboard sleeves and paper boxes. Ask us for eco-friendly options for your product.

How can I contact you?

WhatsApp or call us on +92 301 7090822, email nomanmazhar643@gmail.com, or use the contact form.
