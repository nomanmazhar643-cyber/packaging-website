About PackCraft

Custom packaging for cafés, restaurants and brands, from Multan, Pakistan.

Who we are

Packaging that works as hard as your product

PackCraft supplies custom packaging from Multan, Pakistan: boxes, paper cups, coffee and burger sleeves, food packaging and paper products.

Each product is available plain or printed with your logo and colors. Tell us what you are packing, the size and the quantity, and we will suggest the right option and send you a quote.

  • Boxes: rigid gift boxes, food boxes and pizza boxes
  • Paper cups: hot and cold cups, bowls and lids
  • Sleeves: coffee, burger and chocolate bar sleeves
  • Paper & folders: cheese paper and presentation folders
